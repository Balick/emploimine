General informations

Present in the DRC for more than 70 years, Africa Global Logistics DRC meets major local logistics challenges and in neighbouring countries every day.

With offices in the country's main cities (Kinshasa, Lubumbashi, Goma, Matadi, etc.), its headquarters are based in Kinshasa and manage more than 30 thousand m² of warehouses and storage space.

Africa Global Logistics DRC is also present at Luano International Airport with an office to facilitate its customers' requests.
